# Datasheets and standards

Third-party documents the designs refer to. Their publishers hold the
copyright; see [`../../LICENSING.md`](../../LICENSING.md). The paper's
reference numbers are given so its citations can be followed.

## Kept here

Component datasheets, as the 2019 and 2020 projects carried them (the 2020
files were uploaded to the [OSF project](https://osf.io/me9a8) on 2020-04-09;
the 2019 files are from
[OS Nano Balance](https://github.com/brhubbar/OS_Nano_Balance), and the three
they share are byte for byte the same).

| File | What it is | Publisher | Paper ref. |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `TAL220.pdf` | TAL220 parallel-beam load cell (M4/M5 threads), 3–200 kg | HTC-Sensor | [78] |
| `TAL221.pdf` | TAL221 miniature load cell (M3 threads), 100–1500 g | HTC-Sensor | [79] |
| `hx711F_EN.pdf` | HX711 24-bit ADC for weigh scales | Avia Semiconductor | [63] |
| `GDM1602K-Extended.pdf` | GDM1602K 16 × 2 character LCD module | Xiamen Ocular | [62] |
| `1602LCD.pdf` | 1602A 16 × 2 character LCD module instructions, the 2019 project's LCD reference (SparkFun's copy) | unnamed module maker (2012) | — |

## Linked, not kept

Two Scale Manufacturers Association documents were uploaded to OSF beside the
datasheets and were in this folder until 2026-09. They are the association's
publications, not component datasheets, so they are linked instead. The SMA's
site, <https://scalemanufacturers.org/>, no longer serves them at the paths
the paper cited; the Wayback Machine has copies, and both remain in this
repository's history (tag `mtu-osf-2020`).

| Document | Where | Paper ref. |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SCP-0499 Scale Serial Communication Protocol (1999), which the 2020 firmware implements to Level 2 (`ScaleCommProtocol5199M1.pdf`) | [scalemanufacturers.org/PDF/ScaleCommProtocol5199M1.pdf](https://scalemanufacturers.org/PDF/ScaleCommProtocol5199M1.pdf), archived at the [Wayback Machine (2024-08-06)](https://web.archive.org/web/20240806161954/https://scalemanufacturers.org/PDF/ScaleCommProtocol5199M1.pdf) | [65] |
| Load Cell Application and Test Guideline (April 2010) (`loadcellapplicationtestguidelineapril2010.pdf`) | [scalemanufacturers.org/PDF/LoadCellApplicationTestGuidelineApril2010.pdf](https://scalemanufacturers.org/PDF/LoadCellApplicationTestGuidelineApril2010.pdf), archived at the [Wayback Machine (2024-11-30)](https://web.archive.org/web/20241130072414/https://scalemanufacturers.org/PDF/LoadCellApplicationTestGuidelineApril2010.pdf) | [61] |

Two further documents that were on OSF, the operator manuals of the Denver
Instruments A-160 and APX402 balances used for comparison, were never kept
here; [`../sources.md`](../sources.md) says where they are.
